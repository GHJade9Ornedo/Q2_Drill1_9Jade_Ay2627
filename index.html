🏐 Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Learn the basics of volleyball!

What is Volleyball?

Volleyball is a team sport where players hit a ball over a net. The goal is to make the ball land on the enemy's side of the court.

The Basic Volleyball Skills

1. Serving

Hitting the ball to start the game.

2. Passing

Receiving the ball and sending it to a teammate or your team mate getting it once it was hit by you.

3. Spiking

Hitting the ball forcefully over the net.

Basic Rules

  • Each team has 6 players on the court in indoor volleyball.
  • A team can touch the ball up to 3 times before sending it over the net.
  • Players must not catch or hold the ball.
  • A team scores when it wins a rally.
